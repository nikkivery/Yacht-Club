Sochi Race Week 2020: Event Highlights

More than a hundred participants gathered along the Sochi coastline to compete for the top prize. The regatta brought together both newcomers and professionals experienced sailors, many with years of sailing behind them.

The entire week was blessed with sunny weather, with winds reaching up to 20 knots on certain days. The event was exceptionally well organized, bringing together 135 sailors, 7 crews, and participants from 9 countries across 4 race days and 3 divisions.

Participating teams:

  • Vostok 35
  • Fortune Sailing
  • Siberia
  • Leviathan
  • Black Swan
  • White Pepper
  • Pearl

Fortune Sailing claimed the championship title at Sochi Race Week 2020. After narrowly missing victory last year, the team came back stronger and secured first place, finishing ahead of their closest rivals — the “Vostok 35” crew — by 12 points 5 points.

“We’re a very close-knit team and understand each other without words — that’s what made the difference.”

Congratulations to the winners! We look forward to welcoming all participants again at the autumn regattas.

Artem Kolesnikov June 20, 2021
